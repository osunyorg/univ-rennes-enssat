Research at Enssat: nationally recognised research at the heart of digital technologies

body: >-


ENSSAT (École nationale supérieure des sciences appliquées et de technologie), a public engineering school of the University of Rennes located in Lannion, France, hosts teams from three CNRS joint research units: IRISA, Institut FOTON and IETR. Their work covers photonics, telecommunications, computer science, data, artificial intelligence, and signal and image processing. 

At a glance


Three host laboratories: IRISA (CNRS UMR 6074), Institut FOTON (CNRS UMR 6082), IETR (CNRS UMR 6164, Multip team).

Fields: photonics and optical telecommunications, databases and artificial intelligence, speech and language, communicating objects and energy-efficient computing architectures, unsupervised learning for images and multimodal data.

Long-standing industrial partner: Orange, alongside the school since its creation in 1986.

Link with teaching: laboratory projects in the third year, research master’s, doctorate.

From the photon to artificial intelligence, on a single campus

ENSSAT’s research teams cover a large part of the digital technology chain: the physics of optical components and systems, networks and communicating objects, computing architectures, data management, artificial intelligence, speech and language. This continuity of expertise makes it possible to address digital challenges end to end. 

Our laboratories
IRISA - Institute of Research in Computer Science and Random Systems (CNRS UMR 6074)
Founded in 1975, IRISA is a leading research laboratory in Brittany in computer science, automatic systems,
and signal and image processing. It brings together about 850 people, 40 teams and 7 departments, and
associates eight institutions: CentraleSupélec, CNRS, ENS Rennes, IMT Atlantique, Inria, INSA Rennes,
Université de Bretagne Sud and Université de Rennes. At ENSSAT, IRISA teams work on databases and AI,
speech and language, energy efficiency of communicating objects, and reliable, energy-efficient computing architectures:

Institut FOTON — Optical Functions for Information Technologies (CNRS UMR 6082)
Institut FOTON is the research unit of Enssat’s photonics researchers. It operates under the CNRS and the University of Rennes, and ENSSAT’s teams work there alongside colleagues from the IUT of Lannion, the University of Rennes and INSA Rennes. Its topics range from photonic materials and components to laser physics, complex optical functions and optoelectronic sensors, covering the optical layer “from the atom to telecommunications systems”. FOTON has about 130 people, including about 60 in Lannion in the Photonic Systems (SP) team.  At ENSSAT:

Department of Photonic Systems (Foton Laboratory, UMR CNRS 6082) Director of the Institute: Mehdi Alouini Head of Department: Yannick Dumeige

IETR — Institute of Electronics and Digital Technologies (CNRS UMR 6164), Multip team

The Multip team conducts research on unsupervised learning to develop analysis and processing systems that
support decision-making from n-D images and multimodal data.

Partnership-driven research

Orange and ENSSAT have worked together since the school was created in 1986. In February 2024, Lab’Optic , a joint laboratory dedicated to optical telecommunications, was launched with Orange, ENIB and IMT Atlantique, building on the Orange, Institut FOTON and Lab-STICC laboratories. Enssat’s research is also part of the Anticipa technopole and the Photonics Bretagne cluster ecosystem. 

Research that trains engineers

ENSSAT’s teacher-researchers are members of nationally recognised laboratories. Their research keeps courses up to date and lets engineering students take part in laboratory work, notably in third-year projects. Graduates can continue with a research master’s and then a doctorate.
