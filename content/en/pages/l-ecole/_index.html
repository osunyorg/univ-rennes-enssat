ENSSAT is a French public engineering school affiliated with the University of Rennes, based in Lannion (Brittany). It trains engineers in photonics, electronics and computer science, either through full-time study or apprenticeships, and its graduates find their first job within 1.5 months on average.

body: >-

National Higher School of Applied Sciences and Technology

In Lannion, Brittany (in western France), digital technology and light are part of the landscape. Photonics, cyber security, artificial intelligence, embedded systems, networks: the technologies that are transforming our lives are developed here, in renowned research laboratories and innovative companies located just a few minutes from the campus. ENSSAT places you at the heart of this ecosystem. From your very first year, you’ll learn alongside researchers, engineers and industry professionals who are driving their fields forward. You’ll work in small groups, using state-of-the-art equipment, on real-world projects. You’ll be supported by teams who are close at hand, in a school on a human scale.

Les atouts

Specialisms for the future
photonics, computing, electronics, cyber security, AI & multimedia

Two lanes
To become an engineer : student or apprentice status

World-class research
the laboratories and research teams based at the school , which are recognised both nationally and internationally

Successful integration
On average, it takes 1.5 months to find a first job
