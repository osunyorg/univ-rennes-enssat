A human-sized school

The size of the School (max 120 students) puts you at the centre of the ENSSAT organization. And its "human-sized" character is a real advantage, provided that it does not mean a less demanding school or less connected to the professional world.

More personalized support At ENSSAT, this approach is reflected in particular in a pedagogy in small groups in certain courses and in individualised support. You're not just a file number!


Better proximity to teachers You can more easily: - ask questions outside the course; -

discuss a professional project with a teacher

; -

obtain advice on an internship, academic exchange or project; ... This proximity can also facilitate group projects and collaborative work, which the ENSSAT particularly emphasizes in its training.
Testimony of Alexis, a graduate, who emphasises the cohesion of promotions and mutual assistance between students.

Skills that make a difference

The development and acquisition of knowledge consists in cultivating one's behavioural and relational skills (or soft skills) to better interact with others and adapt to their environment. For
A future engineer is far from anecdotal:
Management
project,
communication with a client,
teamwork,
organization and responsibility...
At ENSSAT, these are precisely skills that are not developed solely with math or computer courses. And the School's teachers are themselves trained in these pedagogical approaches.
