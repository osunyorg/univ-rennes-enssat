Article L713-9 The school forms part of a university (the University of Rennes); it is administered by an elected council and headed by a director, chosen from among the categories of staff eligible to teach at the school, regardless of nationality. School directors are appointed by the Minister responsible for higher education on the recommendation of the council. Their term of office is five years, renewable once.

Organization chart of the Enssat - National School of Applied Sciences and Technology

ENSSAT Council
ENSSAT is governed by the School Council, comprising:


on the one hand, 15 elected members from ENSSAT:

- 4 university professors or equivalent;

- 4 other lecturer-researchers, lecturers, researchers or equivalent;

- 4 student representatives;

- 3 representatives of the BIATSS staff  (Library, Engineering, Administrative, Technical, Social and Healthcare Staff).


and 14 appointed external members, including:

- 3 désignées par des collectivités territoriales : Région Bretagne, Département des Côtes-d’Armor, Lannion-Trégor Communauté ; - 4 représentants d’entreprises ou d’organismes employant un nombre significatif d’ingénieurs ; - 1 représentant d’une institution ou d’un organisme impliqué dans la recherche ; - 1 représentant d'une organisation syndicale d'employeurs ou d'organisme professionnel, choisi par rotation au sein d’une liste établie par le conseil de l’ENSSAT ; - 1 représentant, ingénieur ou cadre, d’une organisation syndicale de salariés, choisi par rotation au sein d’une liste établie par le conseil de l’ENSSAT ; - 1 ancien élève diplômé de l'ENSSAT, désigné par l’Association des anciens élèves de l'École ; - 3 désignées à titre personnel. Le Conseil définit les grandes orientations de l'école, en
particulier le programme d'enseignement et de recherche, ainsi que le
budget. Il propose le nom d'un directeur au ministre pour nomination.
Le directeur, dont le mandat est de 5 ans, a pour principale mission
de mettre en œuvre les décisions du Conseil. Il s'appuie sur les
diverses instances de l'École et des commissions pertinentes si besoin.

Enssat organisational chart
The director prepares the council's
deliberations and ensures they are carried out. The director is the
authorising officer for revenue and expenditure. The director has
authority over all staff. No posting may be made if the headteacher
issues a reasoned adverse opinion. Schools have financial
autonomy to meet the requirements of their development. The relevant
ministers may directly allocate to them funds and posts assigned to the
university.

Organigramme de l'Enssat - École Nationale Supérieure des Sciences Appliquées et de Technologie

Bodies and committees
All Staff Committee
Comprising 10 ex officio members and 7 elected members, this committee is consulted on all matters relating to the running of the school as a whole. Its role is to prepare for meetings of the ENSSAT Council.

Scientific Advisory Board
 It comprises six elected members and three members appointed by the Director. It decides on all matters relating to Enssat’s research policy.

Professional Development Board
It comprises six individuals, selected primarily from the industrial sector for their expertise in the School’s specialist fields. It is responsible for assessing ENSSAT’s degree programmes and their relevance to the needs of the economy and society.

Academic Board
This council is made up of an equal number of lecturers and engineering students, representing the different years and specialisms of the engineering programme, and is consulted on matters relating to the organisation of teaching and student life.

BIATSS Staff Committee
The Committee for Library, Engineering, Administrative, Technical, Social and Healthcare Staff (BIATSS), comprising 10 members, issues an opinion on matters relating to the professional and collective life of BIATSS staff.
